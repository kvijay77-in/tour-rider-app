# Training challenge

_Last updated: 2026-09-29_

The Home card and drawer open a Training Challenge page showing the active tour's `training-challenge` image from the tour artifact manifest, when available, and “Coming soon....” message. The Home card uses the same artifact ID. Images are not bundled and have no bundled fallback; see [inline tour images](tour-and-ride-documents.md#inline-tour-images). Challenge enrollment, tracking, progress and rewards are **not implemented** in this app. Do not advertise those as available functions.
